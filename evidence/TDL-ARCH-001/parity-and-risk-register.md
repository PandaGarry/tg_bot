## 2. Risk Register (Extraction, Asset Pipeline & Baseline Defects)

| ID | Risk / Existing Defect | Severity | Evidence / Exact Location | Impact if Unmitigated | Required Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **Confirmed Baseline Bug: Right-Hold Triggers Pickup** | **High** | `CourtInput` (`CourtScene.tsx:1443–1467`); proven in `smoke-report.json`: `rightHold700msTriggeredPickup: true` | Stationary right-mouse hold (`>=550ms`) during camera orbit picks up buildings/TownHall unintendedly; extracting input logic blindly preserves or worsens gesture conflicts between `CameraRig` (`el.setPointerCapture`) and R3F raycast events | Guard `onPointerDown` timer with `if (e.nativeEvent.button !== 0) return;` (and cancel on multi-touch / `pointercancel`) while preserving left-press and touch long-press |
| **R2** | **TownHall Lv.1–Lv.5 Visual Indistinguishability** | **Medium** *(baseline defect)* | `CourtScene.tsx:1559,1647,1660`; screenshots `01`/`02` vs `05`/`06` | Lv.1 -> Lv.2 changes scale by only `+5%` and adds a tiny gold trim strip mostly hidden by the roof overhang; Lv.4 & Lv.5 have **no** new geometry at all | Ensure Phase D Citadel defines intentional silhouette/architectural changes across upgrade tiers, and keep legacy comparison honest |
| **R3** | **Production HTTP Server Missing Asset/WASM MIME Types** | **Blocker (Phase C)** | `apps/server/src/http.ts:327–336` (`contentType`), `L81` (`Cache-Control: immutable`) | `.wasm` (`basis_transcoder.wasm`, `draco_decoder.wasm`), `.glb`, `.gltf`, `.ktx2`, `.hdr`, `.exr`, `.jpg`, `.webp` are served as `application/octet-stream`; streaming WebAssembly compilation fails and unhashed `public/` assets are cached 1 year | Extend `contentType()` for `.wasm` (`application/wasm`), `.glb` (`model/gltf-binary`), `.gltf` (`model/gltf+json`), `.ktx2` (`image/ktx2`), `.hdr`, `.exr`, `.jpg`/`.jpeg`, `.webp`; serve decoders/assets with content hashes or versioned paths |
| **R4** | **Monorepo Boundary Breakage (`boundaries.ts`)** | **High** | `tools/check/boundaries.ts:90–100` | Importing `CATALOG`, `FOOTPRINT`, or `TH_COSTS` from `@tdl/module-court` into `apps/client/src` fails `pnpm check` | Place shared building contracts (`CourtBuildingSpec`, `FOOTPRINT`, `CATALOG`, `TH_COSTS`) in `@tdl/protocol`, imported by both `@tdl/module-court` and `@tdl/client` |
| **R5** | **Duplicated Catalog / Footprint Drift** | **Medium** | `module.ts:32–69,487` vs `BuildPanel.tsx:22–59` vs `World.tsx:107` vs `CourtScene.tsx:300–313` | Client ghost footprint or removable check silently disagrees with server validation (`court.place` / `court.move` / `court.remove` returns `[]` no-op) | Consolidate to single source in `@tdl/protocol` during Phase B Step 1 |
